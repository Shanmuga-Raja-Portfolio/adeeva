# Maker Studio - Visual Reference Titles (Phase C blind comparison)

Fetched: 2026-10-14. Every data point below was fetched on this date. Purpose: internal side-by-side visual study only. Screenshots belong to their owners; do not redistribute them, ship them, or trace them.

**Method.** Steam data comes from Steam's own endpoints: `store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=<id>&cc=us` for developer, platforms, US price, release date, age ratings, content descriptors and official screenshots, and `store.steampowered.com/appreviews/<id>?json=1&language=all&purchase_type=all` for the review label, percent positive and total reviews (all languages). itch.io data comes from the public game pages (stars and count as shown on the page). Prices are US-store prices at fetch time; sales are noted. No sales or revenue estimates are included. "None shown" means the store page showed no age rating. Steam content-descriptor notes were empty for every Steam title selected.

**Kid-suitability flags.** Tailor Tales is a romance/otome dating sim and is flagged as unsuitable for younger players; it is listed as an alternate only. Cook, Serve, Delicious! 3?! has a satirical "war-torn" setting (ESRB E10+). Everything else looks broadly family-appropriate based on store descriptors and tags. Rejected during search for kid-safety reasons: Dress-up Traveller (sexual-content descriptor), Bonnie's Bakery (blood/cannibalism descriptor), The Chef's Shift (mafia, drugs, mentions of suicide), The Midnight Bakery on itch.io (tagged "weird").

**Thin genres.** At fetch time Steam has no highly rated jewellery-making or furniture-woodworking game with a large review base. The closest titles are listed, plus strong craft-bench analogues (Master Of Pottery, Potion Craft). Several on-genre titles were unreleased or had no reviews: The Woodworking Game, The Woodworker, Furniture Restoration Simulator: Garage Edition, Jeweler Simulator, Master of Jewelry, Jewelry Keeper, Patisserie Simulator (29% of 7), Fashion Designer.
